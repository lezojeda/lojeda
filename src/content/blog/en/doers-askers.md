---
title: "Doers vs askers"
pubDate: 2026-07-16
tags: ["misc"]
---

One of the most important aspects of any social group is the delicate balance of doers vs askers

Both are necessary but if you have way more askers than doers then you have a big problem

Those doing will be overwhelmed by so many requests, questions, plans, etc. from the askers and these in turn will inevitably end up with free time which unless they use it to _do_ like the doers, they'll fill it up with more plans, more requests creating a doom loop that only worsens the things in the social group.

Applies to friend groups, companies, small teams, etc.

Replace doer with executor, operator, worker, developer, implementer

Replace asker with planner, general, commander, project manager, etc.

Many people many times dislike hierarchical pyramids but these structures emerge from objectives themselves, you can't have more askers than doers, it's just that simple.
